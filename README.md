VICE PANELS turns your photos into multi-panel Vice City style poster covers — seven layouts (Character Hero with one big character panel, Hero, Trio, Quad, Split, an angled 5-panel grid, a 3×3 nine-panel grid that never repeats a photo while nine are loaded), seven colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Coast, Noir Teal, a Heatwave gradient-map, a Poster Ink comic halftone shader, and Split Tone), per-panel crop and zoom, a condensed poster-block title that auto-fits the cover with halftone dots and a striped retro sun — and exports them as PNG in square, portrait and wide post sizes, one at a time or all three at once, all offline in the browser.

Nine original sample photos ship with the app (three character portraits — neon queen, Hawaii keys, duo skyline — plus sunset boulevard, neon boardwalk, night skyline, beach couple, classic car, marina dawn), so the first cover already shows a character-led set; load your own photos from disk instead and Character Hero puts your first photo big while every panel shows a different shot.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts, then open http://localhost:8080
```
