VICE PANELS turns your photos into multi-panel Vice City style poster covers — seven layouts (Character Hero with one big character panel, Hero, Trio, Quad, Split, an angled 5-panel grid, a 3×3 nine-panel grid that never repeats a photo while nine are loaded), eight colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Coast, Noir Teal, a Heatwave gradient-map, a Poster Ink comic halftone shader, Split Tone, and Comic Ink — a real canvas shader that posterizes any photo and inks its edges, turning it into comic art), per-panel crop and zoom, a condensed poster-block title in the bundled Anton font that auto-fits the cover with halftone dots and a striped retro sun — and exports them as PNG in square, portrait and wide post sizes, one at a time or all three at once, all offline in the browser.

Thirteen original sample photos ship with the app — five comic illustration characters (ink queen, ink Hawaii, ink duo, ink leather, ink blonde), three photo characters (neon queen, Hawaii keys, beach couple), and five scenes (sunset boulevard, neon boardwalk, night skyline, classic car, marina dawn) — so the first cover already shows a character-led set, and six of the 3 × 3 grid's nine panels are characters, with a scene in the middle-right panel where the striped sun has sky to live in; load your own photos from disk instead and Character Hero puts your first photo big while every panel shows a different shot. The upload slots always show a thumbnail and the name of the photo loaded in them.

The title font is Anton, bundled under `app/fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`); the competition rules name the SIL OFL as a license that allows shipping a font, and the kit's own house apps letter in Anton. Everything in the app — art, lettering, fonts — is original; no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts, then open http://localhost:8080
```
